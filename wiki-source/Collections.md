# Collections

> Collections group resources by medium. They do not collapse a client, server, directory and extension into one recommendation.

## How a collection stays useful

Every collection starts with a role explanation, then links to resource records that preserve canonical origin, evidence label and review date. A collection may mention a discovery source only in its attribution context. The source is not transformed into a blanket endorsement.
