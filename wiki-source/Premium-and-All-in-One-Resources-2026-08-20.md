## Evidence and selection limits

All listed repositories returned a non-archived public record, a declared SPDX license and a 2026 repository activity signal on 2026-08-20. Publication versions are cited only where the canonical release endpoint returned one. The selection deliberately excludes Stremio Shell from the premium list: while its repository exposed a 2026 activity signal, the most recent release observed was dated 2020-06-04, so that signal is not sufficient for this section's current-release emphasis.

The community sources credited in [Sources and Attribution](Sources-and-Attribution) remain discovery context. GitHub repository metadata and release pages are the primary evidence for this specific intake. Community discovery never substitutes for review of source provenance, third-party availability, regional restrictions, account requirements or applicable media rights.

## Use responsibly

Tools that can retrieve or organize media do not confer rights to copy, access or redistribute material. Respect service terms, creator rights and rules that apply where you use a tool. Prefer your own media, authorized downloads and official project distribution channels; do not provide account tokens, browser cookies or personal library data to untrusted extensions or builds.
