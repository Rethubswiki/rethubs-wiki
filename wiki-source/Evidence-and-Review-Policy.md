# Evidence and Review Policy

Rethubs Wiki prefers a project’s canonical repository, release notes, documentation and license file over mirrors, app directories and social posts. A secondary index can help discover a project, but does not establish authorship, maintenance or compatibility by itself.

| Label | Meaning | Editorial response |
|---|---|---|
| **Declared** | The canonical project explicitly states the field. | Link to the statement and record the review date. |
| **Observed** | A reviewer saw the field in a dated canonical source. | State the observation without extending it into a guarantee. |
| **Inferred** | The information is a limited interpretation of evidence. | Explain the basis or omit it where precision matters. |
| **Unconfirmed** | The available source does not support the claim. | Do not promote it as a capability or recommendation. |

## Approval boundary

The review system may gather metadata and prepare a draft, but it does not publish a candidate, change a compatibility claim, or alter an existing Wiki entry without editorial approval. Rejected candidates remain out of the public source. Private review notes, browser-local preferences and personal data are never exported.

## Corrections

Corrections are welcome through a pull request or issue containing the exact field, an authoritative source and a concise explanation. A correction is visible in the Wiki only after its source change is reviewed and merged.
