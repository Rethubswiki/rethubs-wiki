# Rethubs Wiki

> **La Pequeña Ballena Celeste** marks an evidence-aware map of open-source entertainment resources: a place to orient, compare roles and trace what a cited source actually supports.

**Tags:** [orientation](Tags-and-Discovery#editorial-pages) · [reading](Tags-and-Discovery#medium) · [video](Tags-and-Discovery#medium) · [audio](Tags-and-Discovery#medium)

Rethubs Wiki organizes software, extensions, servers, guides, directories and stores for reading, video, comics, graphic novels and audio. It does not turn these distinct roles into a single recommendation. A record describes the material available at its review date; it is not a promise of availability, safety, legality or compatibility in every context.

## Visual language

The companion site uses a marine–stellar identity: deep blue surfaces for orientation, a small whale marker, clear text hierarchy and reduced motion when preferred. The visual language supports navigation; it never communicates a risk or evidence state through color alone. Read [Visual Language and Accessibility](Visual-Language-and-Accessibility) for its limits.

## Before relying on a listing

Open the project’s canonical source, compare its stated platform and role with the entry, then check the **evidence label** and **last review** field. A community index can be credited for discovery, but it does not replace canonical evidence. The [Resource Entry Template](Resource-Template) shows the required record shape.
