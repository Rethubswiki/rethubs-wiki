# Community Participation

Rethubs Wiki welcomes careful proposals that help readers locate open-source entertainment software and its documented ecosystem. Participation should leave a future reviewer enough information to check a claim independently.

## Conduct

Contributors should be respectful, specific, and honest about uncertainty. Describe projects rather than people; do not harass maintainers, users, or other contributors. Do not present a personal preference, a search result, or a directory listing as confirmed compatibility or security advice.

## Proposal standard

A useful proposal identifies the resource, a canonical URL, an appropriate category, and a short explanation of what should be reviewed. Evidence links are encouraged. The editorial team may request evidence, correct a category, decline a proposal, or retain it as unresolved. A proposal is never published automatically.

## Safety and privacy

Do not submit private accounts, tokens, personal contact details, copied installers, or unlawful access instructions. The optional email field is used only for a consented status update when a delivery provider has been configured; it is not shown in the public Wiki or editorial queue.

## Attribution and corrections

Name the source that led to a discovery. Attribution recognizes discovery work; it does not transfer an external directory’s claims into this Wiki. Anyone may propose a correction with a canonical reference and a concise description of the discrepancy.

## Editorial boundaries

Rethubs Wiki records open-source projects, declared resource relationships, and verifiable maintenance context. It does not certify safety, legality, availability, access to third-party services, or a project’s future. Decisions preserve evidence, dates, and uncertainty rather than hiding disagreements.
