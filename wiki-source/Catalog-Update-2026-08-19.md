## Review record

The canonical repositories were checked on **19 August 2026** for the narrow fields displayed above: project identity, observed activity and the licence or role stated by the project. Community inventories and the supplied Android music guide were used as discovery notes only. They were not copied into the catalog as facts.

Platform labels are a navigation aid. They indicate only the support a project declares or that a reviewed source records; they do not prove equal features, installation success, media availability, legality or continued maintenance.

## Where the entries appear

The public Spanish and English catalog surfaces expose these records with their evidence label and canonical link. The Operations edition keeps review and publication separate: no catalog entry is automatically published only because it appears in a discovery guide.

For a correction, use [Contribute and Correct](Contribute-and-Correct) and include the canonical URL, the field being corrected, the observed date and a narrow statement of what the source actually confirms.
