## A low-friction checking sequence

First, open the canonical project. Second, compare its declared platform and license information with the entry. Third, read dependency and installation notes. Finally, check the **last review** date. This sequence does not assess legal status, safety or suitability for an individual situation; it helps reveal what the available sources actually support.
